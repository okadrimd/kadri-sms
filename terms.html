SMS Terms & Conditions

Program Name: Kadri Orthopaedics Post-Operative Patient Education Program

Program Description: Kadri Orthopaedics ("we," "us," "our") offers an SMS-based patient education program for patients undergoing total knee arthroplasty (TKA) or total hip arthroplasty (THA). Messages include pre-operative preparation guidance, post-operative recovery milestones, rehabilitation tips, and general educational content related to your surgical recovery. This program is educational only and does not replace medical advice from your care team.

By enrolling in this program, you agree to the following terms:

  • You consent to receive automated, recurring SMS messages from Kadri Orthopaedics related to your surgical preparation and post-operative recovery education.
  • Message frequency varies based on your stage of recovery. You may receive up to 5 messages per week during active recovery phases.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your messaging plan.
  • You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
  • For help or questions, reply HELP to any message, or contact our office directly (see contact information below).
  • Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of any purchase, service, or treatment.
  • These messages are not intended for emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Supported carriers include but are not limited to AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, and Sprint. T-Mobile is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How You Opt In (Consent Process)

Patients provide consent to receive SMS messages through one of the following methods:

1. In-Office Written Consent: During the pre-operative office visit or surgical scheduling appointment, patients are presented with a consent form that includes an unchecked checkbox and the following language:

"I consent to receive automated SMS messages from Kadri Orthopaedics for pre-operative preparation and post-operative recovery education. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not required as a condition of treatment or purchase."

The patient must check the box and sign the form. The checkbox is not pre-checked.

2. Online Enrollment: Patients may also enroll at https://kadriortho.netlify.app/ by entering their first name and mobile phone number, checking the consent checkbox, and submitting the form. The consent checkbox is not pre-checked and must be actively selected before the form can be submitted.

No SMS messages are sent until consent has been obtained through one of the methods above. The first message the patient receives is a confirmation of enrollment, not a request for consent.

Opt-in data (phone number and consent confirmation) is stored securely and is never shared with third parties.

Privacy Policy

What information we collect: When you enroll in our SMS program, we collect your mobile phone number and your consent confirmation. We may also collect your first name and surgical procedure type for the purpose of personalizing educational content.

How we use your information: Your phone number and related information are used solely to deliver surgical preparation and post-operative educational SMS messages relevant to your recovery. We do not use your information for marketing or promotional purposes unrelated to your care.

Information sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We may use third-party messaging platforms (such as Twilio) solely for the technical delivery of messages, subject to their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.

Data retention: Your opt-in information and phone number are retained only for the duration of your participation in the program. If you opt out by replying STOP, your phone number is removed from the active messaging list.

Security: We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.

No Protected Health Information (PHI): This SMS program delivers general educational content only. We do not transmit, store, or request protected health information via SMS.
