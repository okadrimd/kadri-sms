Post-Operative Patient Education — SMS Enrollment

About This Program

Kadri Orthopaedics offers a free SMS-based education program for patients undergoing total knee or total hip replacement surgery with Dr. Kadri. After enrollment, you will receive text messages with:

  • Pre-operative preparation reminders
  • Post-operative recovery milestones and guidance
  • Rehabilitation tips tailored to your stage of recovery

This program is educational only and does not replace direct medical advice from your care team. Messages are not intended for emergencies — if you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911.
